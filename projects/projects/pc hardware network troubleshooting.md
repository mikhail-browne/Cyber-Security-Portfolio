## Network Troubleshooting

I have also dealt with a number of network and connectivity issues.

One example involved my brother's PC showing that it was connected to
Wi-Fi while applications and websites were unable to establish a
working connection.

I investigated the issue and used Windows Command Prompt to refresh
the computer's DNS information and renew its network configuration.

The commands I used were:

```text
ipconfig /flushdns
ipconfig /renew

After running these commands, the connection was restored and the PC
was able to communicate normally again.

Problem-Solving Process
When troubleshooting an issue, I try to:

1.Identify exactly what is not working.
2.Consider the possible causes.
3.Test potential solutions systematically.
4.Check whether the change has resolved the problem.
5.Research further if the first solution does not work.
6.Verify that the system is stable afterwards.
7.Working on my own PCs has taught me that troubleshooting is often about
understanding the relationship between different parts of a system.

For example, a network problem may not necessarily mean that the
physical Wi-Fi connection is broken. Investigating the software and
network configuration can reveal a different cause.

My hardware experimentation has also taught me to make changes
carefully, test their effects and understand that performance
improvements need to be measured rather than assumed.
