# TryHackMe — Relevant

## Overview

As part of my independent cybersecurity learning, I completed the
"Relevant" room on TryHackMe.

The room simulated a security assessment of a Windows-based machine.
I was provided with an IP address and had to investigate the target,
identify potential vulnerabilities and demonstrate how they could be
exploited within the controlled lab environment.

## What I Practised

- Vulnerability identification
- Web application security
- Web-based attack techniques
- Windows privilege escalation
- Basic reconnaissance and enumeration
- Analysing potential attack paths
- Working methodically through a security assessment
- Documenting findings

## Approach

I began by investigating the target machine and identifying services
and potential entry points.

From there, I investigated vulnerabilities within the web application
and explored possible attack paths. The room required me to think from
the perspective of a malicious actor while remaining within the
controlled TryHackMe environment.

I also worked through a Windows privilege escalation scenario to
understand how an initial foothold could potentially lead to increased
privileges.

## Findings

The exercise demonstrated how multiple weaknesses can sometimes be
combined to progress through an attack path.

It also reinforced the importance of identifying vulnerabilities before
they can be exploited and understanding the potential impact of poor
security controls.

## What I Learned

This room helped me develop a better understanding of:

- How reconnaissance can reveal potential attack surfaces
- How web vulnerabilities can provide an initial attack path
- Why privilege separation is important
- How Windows permissions can affect system security
- The importance of documenting security findings
- How attackers may approach a system differently from a normal user

## Reflection

The biggest thing I took from this exercise was the importance of
thinking logically rather than randomly trying exploits.

I had to investigate the information available, identify potential
weaknesses and consider how different vulnerabilities could connect.

This increased my interest in penetration testing and defensive
cybersecurity and encouraged me to continue developing my practical
cybersecurity skills.

## Platform

TryHackMe

## Environment

Controlled cybersecurity training environment.

No real-world systems were targeted.
